PERADI - LAWASIA Belt and Road Initiative & Employment Law Conference 2025

10-11 August 2025 | Grand Hyatt Jakarta

Updates on BRI Policies and Investment Laws in Asia's Dynamic Markets and Challenges of Employment Law in Asian Countries

REGISTRATION

Join Us in Jakarta, Indonesia, for the

PERADI - LAWASIA
Belt and Road Initiative & Employment Law Conference 2025
Updates on BRI Policies and Investment Laws in Asia’s Dynamic Markets
and Challenges of Employment Law in Asian Countries

Hosted by PERADI, (Indonesian Advocates Association), this one-and-a-half-day powerhouse event brings together the best minds from LAWASIA’s Belt and Road Committee and Employment Law Committee — all on one dynamic platform.

What to Expect:

  • In-depth sessions exploring BRI Policies and Investment Laws in Asia, as well as Employment Laws and Regulations across Asia
  • Timely insights into the legal and economic developments shaping our region
  • Real-world challenges, fresh perspectives, and actionable takeaways

Whether you're navigating cross-border employment issues or exploring the legal framework of the Belt and Road Initiative, this is your chance to connect, collaborate, and grow alongside leading practitioners and thought leaders from across Asia and beyond.

Conference date:    10 - 11 August 2025

Optional post-conference tour:    Tuesday, 12 August 2025

Location:    Grand Hyatt Jakarta, Indonesia
